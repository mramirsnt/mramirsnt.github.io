# Amir Sanati

I am a Computer Engineering graduate with research interests in Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and AI applications in healthcare. My work focuses on developing intelligent systems for medical image analysis, improving diagnostic accuracy, and studying robust machine learning methodologies for real-world healthcare challenges.

I previously worked as an AI Research Assistant at the Laboratory of AI and Intelligent Health at Persian Gulf University, where I contributed to multiple research projects involving medical imaging, Vision Transformers, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), statistical learning.

My research experience includes:

* Deep learning approaches for automated diagnosis of Pneumothorax using Vision Transformers and attention-based architectures.
* Smartphone-based Pharyngitis diagnosis using image preprocessing and augmentation techniques.
* Statistical learning methods involving wavelet shrinkage and Stein’s unbiased risk estimation.
* A large-scale systematic review and categorization of over 300 scholarly publications in AI-based healthcare diagnostics.

I am particularly interested in:

* Medical Image Analysis
* Deep Learning and Computer Vision
* Vision Transformers and Attention Mechanisms
* Statistical Learning
* Trustworthy and Responsible AI
* Reducing data requirements in machine learning systems
* Bias mitigation in healthcare AI systems

My goal is to contribute to research that improves the reliability, fairness, and accessibility of AI systems in medicine and healthcare.
