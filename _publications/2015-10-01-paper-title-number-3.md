This paper presents a framework for automated chest X-ray report generation using a finetuned Vision-Language Model (BLIP) enhanced with pathology-guided prompts and shortcut bias mitigation strategies.

The study focuses on improving both the accuracy and trustworthiness of generated radiology reports without modifying the underlying architecture or introducing multi-objective training. Pathological labels are incorporated as natural language prompts to guide report generation and improve textual coherence while reducing shortcut learning behavior in the model.

This work explores reliable and explainable AI approaches for medical image understanding and automated clinical reporting systems.
